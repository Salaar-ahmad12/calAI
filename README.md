#CalAI — AI Calorie Tracker
Snap. Analyze. Track. Repeat.
CalAI is your intelligent nutrition companion that turns your camera into a personal dietitian. Simply photograph any meal and our AI instantly identifies every ingredient, calculates calories, and breaks down your complete macro profile — protein, carbs, and fat — in seconds.
What makes CalAI different:
Most calorie apps make you search, scroll, and guess. CalAI just needs a photo. Our Claude AI Vision engine analyzes your meal with restaurant-grade accuracy, so logging food takes less time than actually eating it.

Key Features
📷 AI Food Recognition — Photograph any meal, snack, or drink and get an instant nutritional breakdown powered by Claude AI. Works on home cooking, restaurant dishes, packaged foods, and everything in between.
🍩 Calorie & Macro Tracking — See your daily calories consumed vs your personal goal with a beautiful real-time progress ring. Track protein, carbs, and fat with color-coded macro bars that update as you log.
📓 Smart Food Diary — Every meal is organized automatically by breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner. View your full nutrition history, delete entries with a long press, and watch your running daily total update live.
💍 Macro Progress Rings — Three elegant SVG rings show exactly how close you are to your daily protein, carb, and fat targets at a glance.
📊 Weekly History Charts — Visualize your calorie intake across the week with color-coded bar charts. Green when you hit your goal, orange when you go over — your patterns become impossible to ignore.
🔥 Streak Tracking — Build consistency with a daily logging streak. Seven-day streaks, thirty-day streaks — CalAI celebrates every milestone and keeps you accountable.
⚡ Quick Add — Common items like a banana, latte, or protein shake log in one tap — no camera needed.
